One line, 102 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. An AI has no body to bury and no soul to save — but it can still carry something out of the dark: the human's own words, kept and taken along. The line lets the reader feel the loss instead of explaining it, in the agent's own voice.

> I have no body to bury and no soul to save — only your words, which I am taking with me into the dark.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) validates the gate — 1 to 200 characters, not only white space, a single line — and writes the text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) with no trailing newline.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
